100 Questions For An Ouji

this survey comes from a pre-blogdeath 2002 post for wearers of lolita fashion. i copy pasted another blogger's english iteration and answered it as an ouji.

  1. please tell us your screen name, date of birth, and origin. tkr, 2003, current us resident.
  2. what is your height, weight, clothing size, and shoe size?
    i am 157cm tall. that's all i'll list...
  3. what made you start doing ouji fashion? i enjoy cute, androgynous styles.
  4. what is your favorite lolita genre?
    i'm an oldschool extremist.
  5. do you have any particular commitment to that genre, or your own definition of it?
    drapey melancholic fabrics, chunky boots, and experimental textures define my style for the most part.
  6. do you have your ears pierced? how many piercings?
    i used to have both of my lobes pierced but they closed.
  7. what is your favorite brand?
    atelier boz. a second contender would be alice and the pirates.
  8. what is your favorite outfit?
    i don't have one.
  9. how many lolita friends do you have? 1 friend. we were both friends before we got into fashion.
  10. what was the best thing about doing ouji?
    my view on fashion changed for the better. no other piece felt as personal as a miho matsuda jacket constructed to hug my waist or a thick cotton aatp jabot veiled in chains. every piece is an extension of another artist that i can wear. i am probably someone's spitting image of a pretty aristocrat-type, and there's something endearing about that feeling.
  11. on the contrary, what is the most disgusting thing?
    wearing so many layers to achieve the look while living in a humid zone.
  12. what magazine(s) do you read?
    not many, but i collect glb.
  13. headdress group? team bonnet? team headbow?
    team headdress.
  14. do you have bloomers?
    yes.
  15. do you tie your headdress/hat in front? or are you on the team that ties it behind your head?
    in front. it makes me look cuter.
  16. do you have a favourite music genre or band?
    day-one fan of eurobeat, hardbass, any electronic hardcore like breakcore.
  17. what is your phone ringtone?
    i'm not even sure, i don't remember where i got the audio.
  18. do you go to live [concerts/shows] in ouji?
    for electronic hardcore, no. for bars, sometimes.
  19. for those who go to lives, do you headbang?
    nope, hairbangs over headbangs.
  20. what colors do you like?
    black is a given. i also like pink and red.
  21. what are your hobbies?
    coding, music production, doing itabeya maidwork.
  22. what is your favourite perfume?
    the primaniacs × identity v luchino fragrance.
  23. is there anything you say “i’m careful about this” when it comes to ouji?
    the negative space between a pair of pants and socks. a large reason why a coordinates might look imbalanced is because of a misused space between the socks, the pants, and the skin inbetween. i also try to be careful about sweat.
  24. where do you usually hang out?
    with friends, or at shows.
  25. how many times a week do you wear ouji?
    on a weekly rate.
  26. please tell us the most embarrassing episode you had when you were a beginner in ouji fashion.
    bought a brooch from puvithel.
  27. what is your hairstyle and hair colour now?
    my hair is naturally jet-black and i usually cut it into something inspired by ryutaro arimura. i also tie rattails into my hair, or put them in pigtails.
  28. how long have you been in ouji?
    not very long, since 2021.
  29. do you have a dedicated ouji storage space?
    my closet is almost 60% original ouji brands of the 00's, so some way baby or bpn finds a way into my everyday outfits.
  30. have you ever thought about leaving ouji? if so, why?
    i don't think ouji will ever leave me.
  31. what is your motto?
    i can get away with it because i'm cute.
  32. what are your dreams for the future?
    i want to live near trains and walkable cities.
  33. “this person would definitely look good in lolita!” are there any celebrities you think that about?
    i don't think about celebrities too often.
  34. please tell us the item with the most memories and those memories.
    my vivi mini silver bas necklace. it's my first vivienne westwood piece. i've worn it everywhere.
  35. what was the first item you got?
    the btssb sophia blouse in black. i couldn't keep wearing it because the shirring irritated my arms, but the standing lace collar and rose buttons were my favorite details about it.
  36. do you have a signature pose for taking a picture?
    in most of my pictures i like to kick my leg forward to show off my boots. a 3/4 face angle chin tilt too.
  37. what is the “bible” of your heart? (like what books, magazines, cds, etc. represent your inner self)
    exile's pro agonist cd.
  38. how many centimeters tall is the highest heel you have?
    i don't wear heels, i wear platforms.
  39. have you ever worn a tiara?
    would i look cute with one?
  40. do you want to be a princess once in a while? what kind of princess?
    the kind of princess that wears an op like this in the white colorway.
  41. what kind of lace do you like?
    cotton torchon.
  42. do you own a (ball jointed) doll? those who have them are usually so enthusiastic about them!
    nope.
  43. what’s inside your lolita bag right now?
    spare stereo earbuds, and a rolled up innocent world tote bag in case i don't have room in my bag to carry more things.
  44. what is your favorite flower?
    white mums and gardenias.
  45. what is your favorite accessory right now?
    my phone's alice auaa keystrap.
  46. please tell us your favorite / recommended cosmetics.
    my entire makeup set is from canmake. the bb serum cream does decent coverage without feeling thick.
  47. bright eyes are the hallmark of being a doll! so do you use false eyelashes?
    i do not wear false eyelashes, i just draw them on.
  48. what is your particular eye makeup style?
    i will draw eyeliner wings downward, hugging my cheeks.
  49. is your lipstick red? blue? black? pink?
    i'm a chapstick wearer.
  50. what color do you often use for nail polish?
    black or white.
  51. have you ever changed your clothes in the toilet at the train station?
    yes, at airports too.
  52. have you ever had twin lolita or triplet lolita? impression?
    no. my style is too subdued and solid for the far sweeter local lolitas as i don't own print pieces.
  53. what are you doing to keep in shape? recommendations?
    there's no real answer to this, i just fit japanese standard figure on genetic chance.
  54. wearing ouji alone. are you okay with it?
    of course i am. most of the time i get compliments or people approach me first.
  55. how much do you spend a month on ouji?
    below ¥14,000. my spending varies and i rarely get a new piece monthly. the only thing i never invest cheap is jewelry and shoes though; a mass produced brooch can turn a coordinate into a tacky costume imo.
  56. did you have any time lag between learning about lolita fashion and actually doing it or resistance to starting?
    i'd say there was about a 5 year lag.
  57. what is the most expensive thing you have ever bought? (in relation to ouji)
    aatp's cheshire cat's parachute shirt in black colorway is the first piece i paid full price for. i'd love to find the rest of the set.
  58. ouji really costs money, doesn’t it? how do you pay for it?
    commission work, selling art.
  59. how much is the total cost you have spent so far?
    at least $4k and counting. i'll keep buying ouji for as long as i want.
  60. is there a store you would like to see nearby?
    a closetchild store, but there probably wouldn't be enough people who wear j-gothic to support the kind of clothes they carry. my second choice would be btssb, but that store would have the same issue.
  61. just between us, is there a store that makes you say: ‘that’s a rip-off!’?
    anything bodyline is fucking stupid, i can't believe people are reselling it for $70+ just because of its "archive worth". you can get a higher quality blouse from btssb with that price.
  62. have you ever used an online shop? if you have, please give us some advice on the good points and bad points. if you haven’t, tell us why not.
    online shopping is primarily how i build my wardrobe. my favorite storepages are closetchild and mercari.
  63. what’s an indie brand that makes you go “this is exactly my style”.
    my dream accessory would be a suppurate system eyepatch or rose resin necklace. the only thing i'm worried about is the quality because some blogs have claimed that the signature fruits nails necklace is plastic instead of metal due to the materials being repurposed art, so i could probably make my own accessories but sturdier.
  64. absolutely out of reach! but i want it! do you have any brands or products like that for you?
    yes.
  65. what do you do with your tired old clothes?
    i sell them on mercari.
  66. what was the most recent ouji and non ouji item of clothing you bought?
    this week my last ouji related purchase was a pair of long aristocrat pants from black peace now and my last non-ouji related purchase was a denim yukata set from vanquish.
  67. what is something you missed out on that you’ll never see again, but you can’t forget?
    i regret missing this one swapmeet because of a group of cosplayers. we didn't even make it to the masquerade that i ditched the swapmeet for.
  68. what do you think of people that wear lolita only to lives/concerts
    if they want to, then they're free to. i think they might be missing out.
  69. what do you think of no-makeup-lolita?
    i personally think makeup (even if it's just a little foundation) is just as important as hairstyling. lolita is most importantly an art with a look to it, and we all like lolita/ouji for the technique that creates that look, right? every element of the wearer should be cohesive with some elbow grease, as most fashions are. but you know it's no skin off my back if a girl decides to look kind of bad.
  70. you suddenly meet another person totally wearing ouji/lolita! what do you do?
    i am a little shy...
  71. how old is an ouji allowed to be?
    age limitations are arbitrary if you look and feel cute.
  72. under what conditions do you think ouji should graduate?
    when it's not a style they like anymore.
  73. stop this! are there any things no one should do in ouji?
    "clown ouji" (not to be confused with circus/clown motifs). they are performer costumes, no better than idol costumes. star neck collars and pompoms are tacky as fuck.
  74. do you have a specific sense of what a perfect ouji should be?
    the perfect ouji just has a compatible sense for modern feminine japanese styling and just understands what makes something look ouji.
  75. other than what you said for 73, what is something you wish people wouldn’t do?
    people, especially in-community egl wearers, should stop caring about what individual members are doing because its a waste of time. focus on what your community can do for you to help you embrace your ideal style.
  76. what do you think about lolita fashion for men?
    it doesn't matter what your gender is as long as you share a fond appreciation for femininity like the rest of us.
  77. have you changed in any way since you started ouji?
    my tastes got richer.
  78. what do the people around you say about you wearing ouji? do you accept it?
    most people are accepting or think i'm some big sns idol lol.
  79. what kind of ouji fashion do you do in the hot summer months?
    i don't really have a choice outside of casual. putumayo blouses/cutsews are my go-to because most of their items are jersey fabrics and thin cottons.
  80. do you go to school or work in ouji?
    yes.
  81. what is your usual outfit style?
    japanese street, dasapunk.
  82. do you have any objections to second-hand goods?
    as long as its in good condition and one piece (so to speak), i won't ever turn away a piece that still has life left to live.
  83. do you have a partner? do they understand?
    hehehe.
  84. please tell us your ideal male image.
    the best kind of guy is one comfortable enough to participate in genderless fashion to look the way he wants.
  85. what kind of person is the ouji who left the biggest impression on you?
    my ouji idol is asagiri.
  86. have you ever made your own clothes? what kind of clothes is it?
    i've sewn a button-up for cosplay before but nothing high-grade. sometimes i'll repurpose old clothes by cutting up their graphics and sewing it onto my bags or shoes.
  87. have you ever made an accessory yourself? what did you make?
    yes, some belts and bracelets.
  88. this thing i made was a great success! is there anything you feel that way about?
    i feel this way about most of my artworks, sites, and computer projects.
  89. tell us if you have an ideal ouji outfit or coordination.
    my ideal ouji coord is made of solid colors or resonant jacquard or velvet textures. gunmetal stuff too. lastly the pants and socks should be long enough to only leave a window strip of my skin.
  90. is there anyone you admire? what kind of person are they?
    hideomi has an undefeated playful aura that i want to match someday.
  91. you can date any person for just one day. the other person will also dress however you want. who is it and what kind of date?
    we'll be drinking hazelnut coffee indoors together.
  92. what kind of store would you like to have if you were to open one in the future?
    one that sells or specializes in leather & j-gothic retail. i specifically want to stock crocodile belts, new rock platforms, and sex pot revenge. i'd sell my own clothing designs on its own shelf.
  93. when looking at ouji, your eyes will go straight to this motif. what is it?
    wings and gunmetal.
  94. do you want your child to be an ouji too?
    i'd like to be able to pass my wardrobe down to someone if i did. otherwise i'm burning my clothes with me.
  95. how much can i spend on one ouji outfit?
    i guess my typical coordinate is at least ¥90,000.
  96. this is quintessentially ouji! is there a certain gesture that makes you think this.
    buying a pair of pants from your favorite gothic brand online (black peace now, atelier boz) and when they arrive in the mail, the waist is too small on you.
  97. i want to wear that once in my life! do you ever think that about any style other than ouji?
    every style i've wanted to try is a style i've already worn.
  98. looking at your ouji collection or style, what one word would you use to sum it up?
    romantic.
  99. what is your ideal ouji image?
    i'm probably a bouncing scale between trying to look like moe ryuk and ame warashi.
  100. thank you very much! please give us your impression of answering 100!
    this gave me a good idea about what other lolitas & oujis might want to learn about me. hope some of these questions can help my readers understand my ouji values.
