PVC Collection

I'm addicted to the way vinyl fabrics bunch up and amplifies form, and so I've taken to use my body as a canvas further expressing this fascination since painting it just isn't enough.

I'm mostly into plastic for showing myself off and marinating in the sweat I make when wearing it, and there's no better way to do that but through the glossy, resistant rub of vinyl plastic. I've also gotten compliments when I wear these pieces at the after hours, so I've been chasing the feeling of being admired like a plastic doll.
