Bought from an Atelier Pierrot pop-up event. This type of lace looks best as armwear, especially gloves. I've worn these a few times already for a full day each if that is testament to its comfortability (100% nylon).

The wrist-bows are jacquard with rose details, and the glove has a lace ring worn on the middle finger.
