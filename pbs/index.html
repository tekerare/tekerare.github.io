What Is Pierrot Buckshot?

tadc's sixth episode "they all get guns" was dubbed the jax x pomni episode for applying romantic subtext to the ship, hence the "buckshot" part. the "pierrot" part of course comes from pomni's jester motif, and a "buck" is breeding term for a rabbit.

in fandom the commonly used ship tag funnybunny refers to works featuring a character pairing between pomni and jax.

What Is The Amazing Digital Circus?

a woman finds herself adopted into an ensemble of circus game characters who live in the ring.

these weren't non-playable characters she was trapped with, but other humans who have fallen into the same virtual pitfall as her, having trapped themselves through wearing a headset abandoned in a building. barely do they remember much of their original human identity, forgetting their names upon arrival.

all mortal control filter into their digital game vessels; a walking polygon, a fragile ribbon, a resilient ragdolly, an amnesiac king, a toony bunny and so on...

tadc is an independent comedy webseries developed by gooseworx about the absurd everydays of an adult cast living in a digital game (reminiscent of 90's educational kid's ms-dos games) in complete isolation with nobody but an ai ringmaster whom deals countless quests up his sleeves. i don't think it's for everyone, but i find it a very cute show.
