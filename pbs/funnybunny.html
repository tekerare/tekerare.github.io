What Is FunnyBunny

Overview

What eventually solidified Pomni as my favorite character was her assertion, emotional intelligence, and playful side displayed throughout the series, and these qualities were grounded in mostly her interactions with Jax.

A defining quality Pomni leads with that everyone else in the circus doesn't have is her unrelenting curiosity, nosing around the internal depths of her circus neighbors. She shows gratitude to Ragatha's (kinda hopeless) efforts in trying to connect with her, receives advice from Kinger when least expected, and empathizes with Gangle as well as standing up for her when she's at her most stressed.

On the other hand, Jax is reserved by choice and chooses to be a jerk to everyone since frame one. Her history with the rest of the cast spans into years, yet those who have been in the game for longer than she has don't even seem to know much about her private/prior life or any of her avatar gimmicks (like what her avatar does when she holds her breath, why she breaks the fourth wall, etc.) she keeps it that way too, never getting closer to the other characters and actively antagonizing them.

Around August 2025, I went through and coped with a breakup. When I was in that relationship I felt like I couldn't change because the resentment for me ruined my drive to get closer to them and other people, so I just didn't want to get better.

Even though Pomni has seen all the nasty sides of Jax with others and in private, I felt comfort to see Pomni give Jax a chance she wasn't even entitled to, and I feel comforted when she literally fights her to preserve their relationship. She didn't have to empathize with her, but she chose to and that's important to me. This pairing helped sober me up and learn to move forward.

Gummigoo

Gummigoo is a one-off NPC that shows up in episode 2. Previously the bad guy in the Canyon Candy Kingdom adventure, he and Pomni get stuck in an out-of-bounds character model room and becomes self-aware from the original premise Caine wrote him to have.

While the friendship between them is short-lived, Gummigoo is the very first friend that Pomni connects with and despite being the new guy herself, she invites Gummigoo to the living quarters with her and the rest of the cast in hopes that he feels less isolated upon his self-awareness, and maybe lives there with her to share solidarity in how little they fit in.

Most of the series wraps around the human experience and uses "AI" (presented like a race of sentient living entities, not consumer AI irl) to demonstrate that Pomni's humanity completely ignores the body inhibited and extends to how much the other person has in common with other proclaimed humans.

Even though he's an artificial intelligence, Gummigoo feels for a mother he has never met because Caine never planned past what happens at the end of the adventure until the humans complete it. He doesn't have a life outside proximity of the humans just like the humans don't have a life outside of proximity of Caine. Gummigoo and Pomni's interactions show that

  1. Humans and AI are capable of communication and developing new knowledge in the circus.
  2. Humans and AI come in all bodies and shapes, all decided by Caine.
  3. Caine has full access to the humans and AI, managing or allocating their files and having general control over them (can teleport them, etc.).

Even though Gummigoo's mother was never created, it's still something that feels real to Gummigoo, in the same way that the circus may be digital and artificial but still feels real to Pomni.

Later in the series, we also learn that humans can conjure concepts and objects into reality just like the artificial intelligences. At this point, there are more in common between the AI and humans than there are differences: being under Caine's thumb and not being given as much thought past Caine's programming and as a consequence are stripped of autonomy.

Eventually by the end of the series the humans could prove they are capable of overcoming the ringmaster and proved that the hierarchy had no grounds in their world. Without the hierarchy between a ringmaster and his clowns there is nothing that actually establishes what makes the AI and human two separate races besides how they got there (which doesn't really matter, they're both never going to see the light of the macrocosm moving forward).

Given that Pomni actively empathizes with the artificial intelligences, I think it goes to show her problem with Caine was never about his status as an AI. She wasn't stunlocked by the AI and human distinction which explains why she recovered the fastest after the reveal that she and everyone else were instead snapshots of their real life human counterparts' brains.

If you ignored the AI element and shortened your scope down to the humans, even the humans are so different from each other; one of the humans has children, a couple of them are in their early 20's finding stability. Some have good families and some don't. Drawing a line bears no weight in whether they are included in the humanity club.

On the other hand, Jax divorces herself from being seen as human because it's the only way she can accept piloting a body that isn't hers from real life. She looks at her cartoon rabbit body and goes "Then I should be that." Most of all, she's afraid of her mind resembling a body that isn't hers to choose.

If I was in a body that wasn't mine I personally wouldn't care what would happen to it. I'd probably do a lot of reckless things or remind myself "It's not really me that's getting hurt." When Pomni accepts her digital body as her self, Jax chose to get worse not caring where she ended up because she probably told herself "it's not really my body at least."

Because Jax feels this way about her own digital body, she relies the most on the distinction between AI and humans of anyone else in the cast. She doesn't see the AI like Pomni does. She will completely dismiss the NPCs and their feelings for "not being real," most likely because them being real would go against how she generally feels that she's only really human if she was in her original body.

She will talk about the NPCs like they're toys or cause mindless destruction and griefing like any gamer does when given the prompt to, and she even does a lot of brutal things to the other humans which indicates she thought they were also "not real" until episode 8. This shows that what she considers real is not limited to the NPCs, but is just an arbitrary rule she declares onto others based on convenience.

Gummigoo's last appearance/impression on Pomni would be in episode 4, where she says her last farewells and rests her memory of him. After Pomni bonds with and mourns that NPC variation of Gummigoo, that's when Jax begins to display a more obvious interest in her.

Episode 5 : Recap

Jax's interest in Pomni can be subtle for both the cast and the viewers. For years she's devoted to pranking, swindling, and straight up bullying her friends to the point of desensitizing everyone. In this episode, she puts a lot of effort trying to trick Pomni several times into telling her simple details about herself (you know, rather than just asking direct questions). In the President Pomni segment she tries to learn her favorite color by making her defuse a bomb , and in the Noir Bar segment she tricks her into telling her her age by asking if she's old enough to have a job .

How I See Their Dynamic

Pomni and Jax both want to see themselves in each other. Jax seeks escapism (at the expense of others), and Pomni seeks company and a parallel that she can see herself in, whether it's a real human or an NPC.

What I really enjoy about FunnyBunny is that even if Jax doesn't always take Pomni seriously like she should, she sees her as an equal and goes out of her way to involve her in her business when she usually keeps others at armslength (and has pushed others away for years). Episode 6 for example!

She could've just made fun of her or made her feel lame for considering pacifism during a gun brawl, but she doesn't. She teaches her how to lose her realism, wanting to share her fun with Pomni. Jax has become an outlet for her from all the shock and hardships of being stuck in TADC, and is the first person to make her laugh after maybe a week or two in the circus.

Pomni's raw playfulness and shameless curiosity totally captured my heart. Even if she's on the timid side, she's never a doormat and often confronts or has advice to give to those who need it. She's the exact type of person who can handle Jax, and seems to keep Jax in her place. As her equal, she helps bring out her most comfortable self and lets her be "evil".
