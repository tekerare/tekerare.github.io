General Commission Info

  1. a "commission" is the purchase of an image's license, not exclusive ownership. every artwork or design is explicit property of the artist.
  2. a commission's rate is determined according to the time it takes to work on a request, not its technique.
  3. terms of service may change at any time. slot holders will be informed of updated terms during queue.
  4. all payments (USD) are taken through ko-fi unless otherwise specified.
  5. TAT for each piece will vary from a week to months, so request deadlines upon discussion.
  6. price negotiations are allowed.

The Actual Rates

digital painting is $200 minimum rate, $300 max rate. additional characters are base rate + $50, in which this modifier is only accepted in the composition stage (so, don't ask me to add a new character while i'm coloring it in.)

i don't really like to do digital paintings for other people though, so instead i'm offering my packmule service. my sketches are a flat rate of $30 for virtually anything. additional characters are just double the original rate.
