Commission Instructions


more examples can be found here.

1. payment upfront.

this is a skeb-style, which means you will be giving me artistic freedom of your character/subject. you cannot ask me for fixes or edits. card and cash are both acceptable, $15 flat.



2. provide a phone number.

this is the easiest way for me to keep track of commissions and to notify you on pickup.

after the piece has been delivered, these details are deleted. with this in mind, please write your prompt out.



3. send your references.

the commission will be based off of any images submitted.

  1. you don't need to have a reference sheet, i can use concepts or one-off illustrations.
  2. state if this character belongs to you or if it is from a franchise/media. for personal characters, i cannot use images only obtained online.
  3. please read my commission etiquette for what i do and don't draw.


4. wait

i'll complete the commission the day it's requested, and you will be texted where to pick it up.

in the instance where i haven't finished after the event has ended, wait within 2 weeks before asking for an update. if you'd like to receive your piece through mail, provide a mailing address you're comfortable sharing and the recipient's name.



5. post your commission

like how it turned out? feel free to post it on social media and send an e-mail. your experience will be posted on the testimonials page for others to see.
