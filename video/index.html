cyberpsycho august: cool

this guy was really cool (pun unintended). i think i was the first person to grace upsite (well, besides the organizers) so i was keeping a headcount in at the very beginning. a guy shows up and was shuffling during hexxo even though only a few people including me were there, renting seat at the sides just watching.

i think if you go to any show or rave, you should always dance at least once. however i find it way easier to dance when there's at least one or two other people also doing it, and so do a lot of people.

i hadn't realized that shuffler was to perform after hexxo. someone who can initiate dancing so early is for sure someone who really does more than accept the music. i was really curious about the track lineup that would prepared from this type of guy. i'm like "this guy's energy makes me hopeful for the next 5 hours."

we ended up talking after the show and he knows this footage is posted here. to cool reading this: glazing ahead, lol.

yaoi rave dallas 2026

★★★☆☆
of course i dropped everything to go. if you received a free fujochud sticker with this website's address written on it, lucky you.

the first couple sets of the night were good. i didn't know what music they were going to play until getting to the venue btw. if you plan on going to the next one you should expect an even balance of hardstyle, hard techno, nightcore, and jersey club. the nightcore and jersey club stuff aren't really my thing but i stayed to closing set to give the rest of the fujochuds out and to watch the boykissing contest.

happy international yaoi day (8/01). thanks to everyone who talked to me and wanted pictures.
