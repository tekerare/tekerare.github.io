The Swear Off

this isn't the first time i've sworn twitter off either. i only started using it because 3 years ago my ex-girlfriend was a well-liked artist and most of her friends were met through these circles. i want to be included in everything my girlfriend does, so i'm like "maybe returning isn't so bad as long as i start new and don't step on any toes."

after our break-up, i didn't know how to really handle being on my own. i was stuck on all the promises we made with each other and how i branded myself for her, and how she'd do all these things she said she'd do to make sure i stayed with her (and i did like it; the idea of someone showing so much intent and will to keep me around). i really didn't think someone who can say all those things could lose interest in me. but sometimes that happens.

i was alone that entire time i was with her just like i am now. there's not really much tangible obligation an online partner can give you besides sending food to your house. she can't speak to you in any sort of way that a hug can. all she can do is text and call. online partner or not, i am still alone.

anyway, something happened last month and all i can really say is that i've never felt happier being alone. where i was once someone who couldn't fathom being nobody's particular person, and after my experience with my first ever "adult relationship", i am now thankful for the chance to sit with my own thoughts... i've been asking for more hours at work so i can get my mind off of it. i've been going to more shows too and just find any chance to stay outside of the house. i feel like too many bad memories happen inside of it for me to stay inside.

i was coerced into something i didn't want to be a part of recently. i asked for advice from at least 5 of my friends who are about 4-8 years my seniors, and even though they tell me that i couldn't have known i still feel horrible. and i can't help but feel it's because of having a public twitter account that i'm feeling this way in the first place.

i think accessibility to online strangers is what got me here in the first place, i'm hurting every day and feel less grounded than before because of the lies. i need to limit them and their ability to talk to me. i'm going to start focusing more on my fansites and my art. just things that i can control completely.
