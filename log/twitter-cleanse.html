Written: 2026.05.30

The Swear Off

i was coerced into something i didn't want to be a part of recently, and i can't help but feel it's because of having a public twitter account.

this isn't the first time i've sworn twitter off either. i only started using it because 3 years ago my ex-girlfriend was a notoriously controversial(?) yet well-liked artist and most of her friends were met through these circles. i thought "i want to be included in everything my girlfriend does. maybe returning isn't so bad as long as i start new and don't step on any toes."

after our break-up, i didn't know how to really handle being on my own. i was stuck on all the promises we made with each other and how i branded myself for her, and how she'd do all these things she said she'd do to make sure i stayed with her (and i did like it; the idea of someone showing so much intent and will to keep me around). i really didn't think someone who can say all those things could lose interest in me. but sometimes that happens.

i was alone that entire time just like now. there's not really much tangible obligation an online partner can give you besides sending food to your house. past that, she can't speak to you in any sort of way that a hug can. all she can do is text and call. so i am still alone with an online partner.

i didn't realize any of this stuff until recently. i wanted to make everything i was in proximity to worse since anytime i tried to become better i just couldn't. i'm very much still like this actually.

something else happened last month and all i can really say is that i've never felt happier being alone. i've been asking for more hours at work so i can get my mind off of what happened. i've been going to more shows too and just find any chance to stay outside of the house. i feel like too many bad memories happen inside of it for me to stay inside.

i think accessibility to online strangers is what got me here in the first place, i'm hurting every day and feel less grounded than before because of the lies. i need to limit them and their ability to talk to me. i'm going to start focusing more on my fansites and my art. just things that i can control completely.
