Pierrot Buckshot

What Is Pierrot Buckshot?

In fandom the commonly used term Funnybunny refers to works featuring a character pairing between Pomni and Jax.

The sixth episode "They All Get Guns" was dubbed the Jax X Pomni episode for its contributing romantic subtext to the ship, hence the "buckshot" part. The "pierrot" part of course comes from Pomni's jester motif, and a "buck" is breeding term for a rabbit.

What Is The Amazing Digital Circus?

A woman is adopted as part of an ensemble of circus game characters who live in the ring.

She learns they weren't non-playable characters, but other humans who have fallen into the same virtual pitfall as her, having trapped themselves by wearing a headset. They barely remember much of their original human identity, even forgetting their names upon arrival.

All mortal control filter into their digital game vessels; a walking polygon, a fragile ribbon, a resilient ragdolly, an amnesiac king, a toony bunny and so on...

TADC is an independent comedy webseries developed by Gooseworx about the absurd everydays of an adult cast living in a digital game (reminiscent of 90's educational kid's MS-DOS games) in complete isolation with nobody but an AI ringmaster whom deals countless quests up his sleeves. I don't think it's for everyone, but I find it a very cute show.
